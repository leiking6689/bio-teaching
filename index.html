01为什么是豌豆？Why Peas?

同样在研究"性状如何传递"，为什么孟德尔成功了，而几乎同时代的达尔文却没有找到遗传规律？
Why did Mendel succeed in finding the laws of inheritance, while his contemporary Darwin did not — even though both were studying the same question?

✅ 成功 · Success

🟣 Gregor Mendel · 孟德尔

1856–1864 · 豌豆 Pisum sativum · 8 年
  • 繁殖快：短世代时间，一年内能完成多代实验。
    Short generation time — multiple generations per year.
  • 后代多：每荚多粒种子，便于统计。
    Many offspring per pod — easy to count and analyze statistically.
  • 性状明显：7 对可区分的相对性状（花色、种子形状…）。
    7 clearly distinct traits — flower color, seed shape, etc.
  • 自花授粉且闭花授粉：天然"纯种"，容易控制杂交。
    Naturally self-pollinating → true-breeding lines; easy to control crosses.
  • 定量思维：用数学比例分析后代，发现 3:1 规律。
    Used mathematics to analyze ratios — discovered 3:1.
⚠️ 未得规律 · Did not find the pattern

🐦 Charles Darwin · 达尔文

1850s–1860s · 家鸽 Columba · 同期
  • 世代慢：鸽子性成熟需数月，繁殖周期长。
    Pigeons mature slowly — long generation time.
  • 后代少：每次通常只产 2 枚卵，统计样本小。
    Usually only 2 eggs per clutch — small statistical samples.
  • 性状连续：羽色、体型多为渐进变异，不易划分类型。
    Traits are often continuous (color gradients, body size) — hard to classify.
  • 交配难控：鸟类行为复杂，难以保证纯种亲本。
    Complex mating behavior — difficult to guarantee true-breeding parents.
  • 关注进化而非机制：达尔文关心"性状为何变化"，而不是"如何稳定传递"。
    Darwin focused on why traits change (evolution), not how they are transmitted.
🔑 关键对比 Key Takeaway： 孟德尔的成功不仅在于"选择了正确的生物"，更在于把生物学的观察变成了数学问题。 豌豆的每一荚都是一组可统计的数据点；而达尔文的鸽子更像一幅幅难以量化的肖像画。
Mendel's success came not just from choosing the right organism, but from turning biology into a mathematical question. Every pea pod was a data point; Darwin's pigeons were more like uncountable portraits.

02Perfect Flower · 完全花的结构

豌豆花是典型的蝶形花冠——同一朵花里既有 10 枚雄蕊也有 1 枚雌蕊，而且它们被龙骨瓣紧紧包在里面。 点击花的各部分认识结构；还可以剖开龙骨瓣看内部！
A pea flower has a typical papilionaceous (butterfly-shaped) corolla: 10 stamens and 1 pistil in one flower, all enclosed by the keel. Click each part — or open the keel to look inside!

🌸 点击花的任一部位，或剖开龙骨瓣 Perfect Flower

豌豆花是完全花（perfect flower）：一朵花里同时具备 10 枚雄蕊（♂ stamen）和 1 枚雌蕊（♀ pistil）。

Pea flowers are perfect flowers: both male and female reproductive organs live inside one single flower.

💡 注意：雄蕊和雌蕊被龙骨瓣紧紧包住——这正是豌豆天然自花授粉、容易保持纯种的原因。点右上角按钮可以"剖开"它！

🌱 自花授粉 Self-Pollination
同一朵花内，雄蕊的花粉（pollen from anther）落到同一朵花的柱头（stigma of the same flower）上。豌豆花在开放前，花粉就已经在龙骨瓣里落到自己的柱头上了。
Pollen from the stamen lands on the pistil of the same flower — in peas this happens inside the keel before the flower even opens.

💡 结果：后代永远是"纯种"（true-breeding）——和亲本长得一模一样！
Result: offspring are always true-breeding — identical to the parent.
🔑 Key 1完全花 = 雌雄同花
Perfect = male + female in one flower
🔑 Key 2龙骨瓣包住器官 → 天然自交
Keel encloses organs → selfing
🔑 Key 3孟德尔人工去雄 → 异花授粉
Mendel removed stamens → crossed

03P → F₁：白花"消失"了？The First Cross · Where Did the White Go?

孟德尔首先要保证亲本是纯种（true-breeding）：紫花的后代永远开紫花，白花的后代永远开白花。然后把两个纯种杂交——F₁ 子一代会是什么样？
Mendel first made sure his parents were true-breeding: purple always bred purple, white always bred white. Then he crossed the two lines — what did F₁ look like?

🤔 预测时间 · Make Your Prediction

紫花纯种（PP 类）× 白花纯种（pp 类）。F₁ 子一代会开什么花？
Purple true-breeding × white true-breeding. What color are the F₁ offspring?

✂️ 豌豆能自花授粉，所以人工杂交要先去掉母本的雄蕊，防止自己的花粉干扰；再把父本的花粉涂到母本柱头上。
Peas self-pollinate naturally, so Mendel had to remove the stamens from the mother first, then dust pollen from the father onto her stigma.

  1. ① 去雄 Emasculate：花蕾期剪掉白花（母本）的 10 枚雄蕊——她无法自花授粉。
    Remove the 10 stamens from the white-flower mother before she opens.
  2. ② 授粉 Pollinate：取紫花（父本）的花粉，涂到白花母本的柱头上。
    Dust pollen from the purple father onto the mother's stigma.
  3. ③ 结荚 Pods form：母本子房发育成豆荚——里面的豆粒就是 F₁ 种子！
    The ovary swells into a pod — the peas inside are F₁ seeds.

🟣 显性性状 · Dominant traitF₁ 中表现出来的性状（紫花）。
The trait that shows up in F₁ (purple).
⚪ 隐性性状 · Recessive traitF₁ 中被掩盖、不表现的性状（白花）。
The trait that is hidden in F₁ (white).
🧪 用孟德尔的"遗传因子"（今天叫基因 / allele）解释：紫花纯种提供 P 因子，白花纯种提供 p 因子 → F₁ 全是 Pp 杂种；P 为显性，所以全紫。白花性状没有消失，只是被盖住了。
Purple parent gives factor P, white parent gives p → every F₁ is Pp. P is dominant, so all are purple — but the white factor p is still carried, not lost.

⚠️ 注意：F₁ 没有出现"半紫半白"或"淡紫色"——孟德尔据此否定了当时流行的"混合遗传"假说（blending hypothesis）。
No lavender, no half-and-half: blending inheritance was disproved.

043:1 是怎么来的？The 3:1 Ratio Discovery

孟德尔最惊人的发现：杂交第二代中，"消失"的性状又出现了，而且比例恒定为 ≈ 3:1。
Mendel's most astonishing finding: in the 2nd generation, the "lost" trait reappeared — always at a ratio of about 3:1.
